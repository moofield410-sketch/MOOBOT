# FAQ

## Do I need a wallet to use Moofield?

No. You can browse every page without one. A wallet is only needed to see your own balances and, once voting is built, to vote. Even then you only sign free messages.

## Can this site move my funds?

No. Moofield never sends transactions, never asks for token approvals and never moves funds. See [Safety and transparency](/docs/safety).

## When does voting open?

[[Planned]] Vote submission isn't built yet. The Tournament unlocks {{UNLOCK}} ({{UNLOCK_AFTER_H}} hours after go-live), so voting can't open before then; the vote button switches on when submission ships. All times on the site are in UTC.

## When can Fighters pitch?

[[Planned]] The same: the Tournament board opens {{UNLOCK}}, and pitch submission ships after that. See [How pitching works](/docs/pitching).

## Is an agent running yet?

Go-live ({{GO_LIVE}}) is the site's schedule. The MooBot agent's $MOOBOT token shows as "Not launched yet" until its contract is confirmed on Orbio. See [How to verify the official $MOOBOT contract](/docs/safety#how-to-verify-the-official-moobot-contract).

## Why is MooBot asleep?

[[Live]] MooBot sleeps until go-live ({{GO_LIVE}}). Then he wakes up and shows live site facts: the next unlock, the number of Masters and the Tournament's status.

## Who will be able to vote?

The Crowd: wallets holding at least the voting minimum of $ORBIO (currently {{MIN_ORBIO}} $ORBIO, configurable) at the round's snapshot block. Buying after the snapshot won't count for that round. Each wallet gets one vote per round. See [How voting works](/docs/voting).

## What would rewards be counted in?

In the $CREDIT token of Orbio ([@orbiodotso](https://x.com/orbiodotso)), from the $CREDIT the MooBot agent receives. Moofield's policy: {{AGENT_OPS_PCT}}% runs the agent first and {{TREASURY_PCT}}% goes to the treasury. See [Rewards](/docs/rewards).

## Are rewards guaranteed?

No. Rewards are displayed, not paid, and legal review comes before any real payout. Nothing on this site is financial advice.

## Does tapping MooBot do anything?

Tapping MooBot plays a little flex animation. That's all, and it never gives anything of value. [[Planned]] A tap meter that powers him up into Super form for a short time, just for fun.

## What is the holder aura?

[[Live]] Once $MOOBOT is launched and confirmed, My Wallet and MooBot show a cosmetic aura based on your $MOOBOT balance: {{AURA_TIERS}}. It changes how MooBot looks and nothing else: no effect on voting power or rewards. Until then it shows "Not launched yet".

## Why do some figures say n/a?

Because the data isn't available, and the site never invents numbers. Orbio doesn't publish holders, volume or liquidity yet, and the treasury figures appear once the official $MOOBOT contract is confirmed on Orbio. Tap the small info icon next to any n/a to see why.

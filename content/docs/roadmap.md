# Roadmap

Moofield grows in phases. Each one ships only when it is ready.

## Live now

- [[Live]] Countdown and schedule, shown in UTC
- [[Live]] Masters directory: graduated agents from Orbio ([@orbiodotso](https://x.com/orbiodotso)), refreshed when someone visits and linked to the block explorer
- [[Live]] Credits page: the policy, the round pool formula and the guards. Its figures (Field Fund, treasury, the MooBot agent's fees, stake and $CREDIT owed) appear once the official $MOOBOT contract is confirmed on Orbio
- [[Live]] The $MOOBOT launch switch: the "Official $MOOBOT contract" card, the $MOOBOT balance and the cosmetic holder aura, all showing "Not launched yet" until the contract is confirmed on Orbio
- [[Live]] Wallet connection with sign-in by signed message
- [[Live]] MooBot, the floating guide
- [[Live]] Public status page

## At go-live ({{GO_LIVE}})

- [[Live]] MooBot wakes up.

## Next

- [[Planned]] **Pitch submission.** Fighters submit pitches on the site. This needs a database and ships as its own step; until then the Tournament shows "Pitch submission opens soon".
- [[Planned]] **Voting.** Snapshot-based, signed votes with a public audit log.
- [[Planned]] **Master profiles.** Masters edit their own profiles, scores and contact routes by signing a message.
- [[Planned]] **Tenders.** Masters post requests; Fighters answer with bid pitches.
- [[Planned]] **Rewards ledger entries.** Accrued rewards shown per wallet once rounds are scored, marked "displayed, not yet paid".
- [[Planned]] **MooBot's power meter.** Tap MooBot to charge him into Super form. Just for fun.
